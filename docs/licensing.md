### Project Gutenberg licence

The Project Gutenberg licence (https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html) allows the
text of public-domain works to be reused freely. Its trademark terms apply only to
redistribution that keeps the "Project Gutenberg" name: a derived work that removes the
Gutenberg header, footer and licence text, and does not use the trademark, may be
redistributed without further conditions. This project strips all of that (see `scope.md`)
and does not use the Project Gutenberg name for the visual novel. The raw download is
gitignored and never redistributed.

## Voice

The German voice track is cut from LibriVox's dramatic reading "Faust: Der Tragödie erster Teil"
(https://librivox.org/faust-der-tragoedie-erster-teil-by-johann-wolfgang-von-goethe/), a
public-domain recording by many volunteer readers. The German text used for alignment is
Project Gutenberg #2229 (gitignored, not redistributed). Readers are credited in the game
(`[voice].credits` in `work.toml`).

generation. They are not photographs or scans of existing artwork.
